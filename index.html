Holes & penetrations valve, head, spout, drain

Measure to the centre of the pipe — across from the left edge of this surface, and up from the floor. Then the cut list tells you where the hole lands on its own tile, which is the number you actually mark. Sizes are starting points: cut to what the valve needs and the escutcheon covers.

Niches & recesses a hole in the field, with its own layout

Measure to the opening — across from the left edge, up from the floor to the sill. The trade rule is to land the opening on grout lines: do that and the inside tiles out in full pieces with no slivers at the edge. Pitch the sill 1/8″ into the shower so it drains.
